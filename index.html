Project detail / GoHighLevel

Connecting the customer journey.

One substantial agency project, built around a quiz, relevant follow-up and organised contact data.

01 · Capture & segment

A submitted quiz starts a branching workflow. Answers determine contact fields, segment tags and CRM opportunity records across five audience segments.

02 · Follow up

Segment tags start tailored email sequences with waiting steps. A separate workflow reacts to email clicks and updates CRM opportunities.

03 · Connect & continue

Inbound webhook workflows create contacts, update fields and tags, and connect to CRM steps. A separate post-purchase flow schedules follow-up emails.

Built within OWI using GoHighLevel’s visual workflow builder. The work involved AI assistance and feedback from colleagues. Client records, private configuration and internal performance data are not shown.
